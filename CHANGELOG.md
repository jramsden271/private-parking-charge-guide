# Changelog

## 1.0 — 13 November 2026

First public version.

- Skill split into a core `SKILL.md` plus reference files (POFA checklists and industry Code; court claim and defence; hearing and CCJ; who's who; cases).
- POFA Schedule 4 paragraph 8 and 9 checklists quoting the statute, plus paragraph 7 (windscreen tickets).
- Industry Single Code of Practice (v1.1, 17 Feb 2025) quoted on notice rules, discounts after appeals, grace and consideration periods, keying errors and the Appeals Charter (Annex F). Notes that the Code binds operators but doesn't override the law.
- Relevant land: railway land became relevant land on 26 Dec 2025 (SI 2025/1256 and 2025/1258); airports, trams and TfL flagged.
- Court defence guidance built around pulling apart the Particulars of Claim; template route (one short facts paragraph) vs fully custom defence.
- Tested with simulated conversations across appeals, debt letters, Letters of Claim, claims, hearings, CCJs, council tickets and Scotland/NI.

### Known gaps
- Transcript links still needed for *CPMS v Akande* and *Excel v Smith*; *Jopson*'s old forum link is dead.
- Scotland: keeper liability (Transport (Scotland) Act 2019, Part 8) not yet in force — watch for commencement.
